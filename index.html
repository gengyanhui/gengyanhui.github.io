01 / Profile

About

A researcher and research leader working at the intersection of AI, information theory, and networked systems.

Dr. Yanhui Geng is the Director of the AI Lab at Huawei Hong Kong Research Centre. He previously served as Director of Huawei Hong Kong Research Centre from 2022 to 2026, and as Director of Huawei Montréal Research Centre from 2017 to 2020. From 2013 to 2017 he was a principal researcher at Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab in Hong Kong.

He received B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees in Electronic Engineering and Information Science from the University of Science and Technology of China, and a Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the University of Hong Kong in 2009, supervised by Prof. Victor O.K. Li. He later worked as a post-doctoral research fellow at HKU and as a senior scientific engineer at ASTRI.

His work spans artificial intelligence, machine learning, information theory, wireless communication, and data-center networking, with publications in NeurIPS, AAAI, SIGCOMM, INFOCOM, and IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking.
